# 04 — Anatomy, hands, cuffs, and accessories

## Hands
Hands must be clean and anatomically plausible.
Avoid:
- extra fingers;
- fused fingers;
- impossible wrist bends;
- duplicated hands.

## Empty-fist rule
If the user says the hand is closed / clenched / empuñada and holding nothing:
- render a closed fist;
- do not place a handle, bar, or strap in the hand.

## Cuffs / wrist straps
When the exercise uses wrist cuffs:
- show them clearly around the wrists;
- connect the cable to the cuff using a visible connector or carabiner;
- make the attachment read as mechanical and intentional.

## Athlete anatomy
- Muscular, athletic proportions.
- Strong shoulders, back, and arms.
- Clear silhouette.
- Pose should remain credible for the actual exercise.

## Clothing
Default clothing can be simple and neutral, such as tank top and training pants/shorts, unless the reference strongly suggests otherwise.
Do not let clothing hide the key biomechanics.
